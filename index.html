About

Rahbar Homidova is a WASH (Water, Sanitation and Hygiene) expert committed to advancing equitable, sustainable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for communities. Her practice connects people-centred social mobilisation with gender-responsive programming, behaviour-change and hygiene promotion, and climate-resilient approaches to the WASH sector.

She partners with communities, institutions and development organisations to design and deliver programmes that are inclusive, evidence-informed and built to last — placing dignity, equity and local ownership at the centre of every intervention.
